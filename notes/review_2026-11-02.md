# Review of the first version (Codex draft), 2 November 2026

The first version is `archive/first_version/manuscript/xns121.tex`, with its scripts in
`archive/first_version/ancillary_outputs/`. The new version is in Overleaf, project
6ae89a4fa80c0605cd016185, a clone of which is at `~/overleaf_xns121`.

## Verdict

The argument of the first version is correct in outline, and every computation it relies on
reproduces. Several steps were unjustified or only asserted. All of them can be repaired, and the
new manuscript repairs them. The odd-class Selmer computation was replayed, not independently
reimplemented. That is the main remaining single point of failure on the computational side.

## Steps that were unjustified as written, and their repairs

1. **Rational points above the CM points** (first version §1.2, `verify_cm_lifts.m`). The script
   only checks that the norm-one subgroup of `F_121^×` acts freely on `F_121`. That the Galois image
   realises this action ("CM reciprocity gives this action") was assumed. It was also not shown
   that `n0 G` is the image of the CM point.
   Repair: Lemma 3.3. Frobenius elements at p = 7, 5, 7, 5, 7, 17, 41 satisfy `g^e ≠ 1` with
   e = 10, 20 or 30, which excludes the non-canonical lifts modulo `Aut(E0)`. Prop. 3.1 c) shows
   that `n0 G` is the only rational point above `j(n0 G)`. Both are in `code/cm_lifts.m`.
2. **Valuation of `j` at the cusps** modulo `p ≠ 11`. The first version argued "as the polar
   multiplicity of j at each cusp is 11" without showing that this survives reduction. Repair:
   Lemma 4.2, using `j = (A+By)/u^11` and the new check that `(A+By)(c)` is an 11-unit
   (Prop. 3.1 b)).
3. **`f_T` gives a homomorphism.** The first version called it "an unramified descent homomorphism"
   without proof, and `K` does not contain `μ_11`. Repair: Lemma 4.3, proved with the Weil
   pairing. It also covers `x − θ`.
4. **Points reducing to `O` in the sieve.** The first version had only "At O one uses the
   normalized leading coefficient". This is essential: without it the six primes leave 10 classes
   modulo 4356. Repair: Lemma 4.5 b) and Remark 4.7, with `CONSERVATIVE_O:=1`.
5. **Descent map.** No reference was given for `D ↦ (Y−b)(D)` being the injective λ-Kummer map.
   Poonen–Schaefer does not apply here, since it needs `p | deg f`. Repair: Schaefer, Math. Ann.
   1998, Thm 2.3 and §3. The injectivity for the twist is now proved in Section 5.
6. **Local image dimensions.** The first version asserted them without a reference. Repair:
   Schaefer, JNT 1996, Lemma 3.8. That lemma has a ratio of component groups, which cancels because
   λ is an endomorphism.
7. **Formal group.** The integrality lemma was stated in coordinates "built from Abel maps", with
   no source for the formal group of the Picard functor of the cuspidal model. Repair: BLR 9.3/1,
   and Lemma 6.1 is restated in log coordinates. For the smooth-disc expansion at depth 1 in the
   ramified field (the computation of `d_v`) the naive integration bound fails (`t^11/11`). It now
   follows from the formal group itself (Lemma 6.2 a)).
8. **Choice of signs at several places.** The first version needed one global square root valid
   at two places (odd) or three places (even). It used a function `ρ` with the loosely stated
   estimate "in fact at least 14". Replacement: the Selmer arguments work at a single prime `v`
   with `v(4−θ)` odd. This is verified at both such primes in `code/single_place.m`, so the sign
   question disappears.

## Replays and independent checks

* Full replay of the first-version scripts with Magma V2.30-1 (`archive/first_version/replay_2026-11-02/`).
  Everything agrees, including the sieve data, which is byte-identical.
* Independent:
  - the sieve (`code/sieve_bruteforce.m`), a brute force over the residues modulo 4356;
  - the even Selmer groups (`code/even_selmer.m`), with public intrinsics only, where the first
    version used a patched copy of Magma's PicnDescent; both give dimensions 5, 5, 4, 4;
  - the CM lifts;
  - the genus (511), computed in Magma;
  - the single-place criteria.
* Not independent: the odd Selmer groups. These were replayed at precisions 80 and 160, and the
  code was read line by line.
